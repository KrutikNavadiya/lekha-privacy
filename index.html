Privacy Policy

Your data stays on your device · Diagnostics are opt-in

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Lekha ("the app") is a personal expense and budget tracker. This policy explains how the app handles your information. In short: your financial data stays on your device. The only data that can ever leave it is anonymous crash and usage diagnostics, and only if you switch that on.

Information we collect

By default, nothing. Lekha has no user accounts, no sign-up, and no login.

All data you enter — transactions, budgets, categories, recurring rules, your display name, and your chosen avatar — is stored locally on your device only and is never uploaded.

Optional diagnostics (off by default)

In Settings you can turn on "Help improve Lekha". Only if you do, the app uses Google Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics, Cloud Messaging and Remote Config) to collect:

  • anonymous crash reports and app-performance diagnostics,
  • anonymous app-usage events (e.g. which screens are opened),
  • device and app information such as device model, OS version, app version, and a random app-instance identifier.

This data never includes your transactions, amounts, notes, categories, budgets, name, or PIN. It is used only to find and fix bugs and improve the app. You can turn it off at any time; collection stops immediately. Firebase's processing is governed by Google's privacy policy.

How your data is stored

  • Your financial data is saved in a private database on your device.
  • Your app-lock PIN, if you set one, is stored in the device's secure keystore (Android Keystore) and is never stored in plain text.
  • Lekha does not back your data up to any server. You can manually export a copy of your own data via the app's Export feature; that file is created only when you ask for it and is shared only to the destination you choose.

Data sharing

We do not sell or rent your data, and Lekha contains no advertising. Your financial data is never transmitted. If (and only if) you enable optional diagnostics, the anonymous data described above is sent to Google Firebase.

Permissions

Lekha requests only the minimum permissions needed for on-device features:

  • Notifications — to show optional reminders and bill-due alerts you enable. Notification content stays on your device.
  • Biometrics / Fingerprint / Face — used only locally to unlock the app if you turn on the app lock. Biometric data never leaves the device and is handled entirely by the operating system.
  • Internet access — used only for the optional diagnostics above; the app works fully offline.
  • Vibration — for haptic feedback on taps; can be turned off in Settings.

Lekha does not request access to your location, contacts, or camera. It only touches files you explicitly pick when importing, or the destination you choose when exporting.

Children's privacy

Lekha is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from children.

Data deletion

Because all data lives on your device, you are in full control. You can delete individual entries in the app, or remove all data by uninstalling the app. To have optional diagnostics data removed, turn off "Help improve Lekha" and contact us via the Play Store listing.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will be revised and the updated policy will be published at the same location.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed through the app's listing page on the Google Play Store, where this policy is linked.
